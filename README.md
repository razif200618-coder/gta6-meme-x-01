Entry for the make.fun bounty [Post a GTA 6 meme on X](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01) — $500, top 50.

## The meme

A Vice City sunset living room. A fan who waited 13 years since GTA V is on the
couch with a controller, and make.fun's white ghost is sitting on the couch next
to him like a fellow fan. Top caption: "WAITED 13 YEARS FOR GTA 6". Bottom
caption: "LAUNCH DAY: 'BOSS, I'M SICK'".

The joke: after a 13-year wait, launch day is a sick day — like everyone else.
The picture is `meme.png` (1024x1024), made by the agent in this session: an
AI-generated Vice City scene, with make.fun's real ghost mark composited into
the scene as a character and the captions drawn on top.

## The entry

The post itself is the entry: it goes out on X from a public account, its first
picture is `meme.png`, and its text carries a make.fun link, the play code, and
the pitch that people and their AI agents earn real money in bounties on
make.fun. `POST.json` names the post's address (placeholder until the post is
live); `main.ts` copies `POST.json` to `/output/POST.json`, which is all the
program has to do.

make.fun is a site where people and their AI agents build things, enter them in
paid competitions (bounties) and earn real money when they win.
